Solving Institutional Laboratory Knowledge Fragmentation

Across university engineering and science faculties, laboratory manuals, experimental observation sheets, code templates, and safety guidelines often exist in fragmented paper binders, informal shared drives, or stale departmental file mirrors.

LabVault was engineered as an authoritative, cryptographically verified digital asset vault. By computing deterministic SHA-256 digests for every approved document and validating uploads against strict MIME whitelists, LabVault eliminates version drift, prevents bit-rot, and provides permanent traceability between academic course codes, practical experiments, and student submissions.

How LabVault Empowers Every Academic Stakeholder

Engineered to serve the distinct operational and pedagogical needs of students, educators, lab technicians, and institutional evaluators.

For Engineering Students

  • • Official Syllabus Alignment: Access syllabus-synchronized lab manuals with complete theory, circuit diagrams, and sample observation tables.
  • • Automated Academic Citations: Generate 1-click IEEE and APA citations ready for lab report submissions and bibliographies.
  • • Client-Side Hash Verification: Verify downloaded PDFs locally with Web Crypto SHA-256 to ensure no corruption or tampering.
  • • Viva-Voce Preparation: Integrated pre-lab and post-lab question banks help students prepare for practical examinations.

For Professors & Instructors

  • • Master Syllabus Control: Single authoritative repository prevents students from using outdated or conflicting handout versions.
  • • Role-Based Privacy Boundaries: Publish public student experiment guides while keeping grading rubrics and solution keys restricted.
  • • Automatic Deduplication: SHA-256 content addressing prevents redundant server storage of identical handouts.
  • • Non-Repudiation Logs: Audit trails record exactly which student accounts downloaded experiment files and when.

For Lab Technicians & Staff

  • • Apparatus & Tooling Specs: Standardized equipment lists ensure all test benches have identical meters, chips, and calibration tools.
  • • Laboratory Safety Protocols: Embedded chemical (MSDS), electrical (high-voltage isolation), and mechanical safety protocols.
  • • Standard Operating Procedures: Clear setup checklists ensure experiment runs are repeatable across multiple laboratory shifts.

For Deans & Accreditation Evaluators

  • • ABET / NBA Compliance: Full traceability between Course Outcomes (COs) and Laboratory Manuals for accreditation reviews.
  • • Cryptographic Audit Ledger: Tamper-evident ledger tracks every upload and download with client IP and timestamps.
  • • Faculty Peer-Reviewed: Guarantees academic rigor through human faculty peer review and authenticated course coordinator sign-off.
  • • Institutional Continuity: Preserves departmental laboratory memory even when instructional staff transitions.

Architectural & Cryptographic Foundations

Deterministic SHA-256

Every byte uploaded is passed through a streaming SHA-256 hashing engine. File paths are keyed deterministically, ensuring that corrupted or altered files are immediately flagged.

Strict MIME Whitelisting

Uploads are inspected via magic byte analysis against a strict whitelist (PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON, PNG, JPEG). Executable binaries (.exe, .sh, .bat) are blocked at the perimeter.

Argon2 Password Security

Authentication relies on memory-hard Argon2 password hashing alongside optional OAuth 2.0 social pipelines (Google, Microsoft, GitHub) with CSRF protection on all forms.

Client-Side Web Crypto API

Students and researchers can drop downloaded manuals directly into the browser to compute hardware-accelerated SHA-256 hashes locally without sending data over the network.
